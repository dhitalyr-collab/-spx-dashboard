Risk summary
*“Real-time” labels refer to the provider’s own displayed status for derived/reference data. Exchange-traded ES, NQ and ZN quotes on free public pages may be delayed because of exchange licensing. Investing.com also states that website data can be indicative and may differ from executable market prices. For an actual SPX/0DTE entry, use your broker’s executable quote and define invalidation before entering.
